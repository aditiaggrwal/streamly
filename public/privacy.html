Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Streamly helps you pick a movie you can watch tonight. We keep the data we store small and tied to that purpose.

If you browse without an account

  • Tonight’s answers (mood, time, genres) stay on this device only.
  • Streaming services are not saved. You’ll choose them again next visit.
  • We do not create a profile for you.

If you sign in

  • We store your email and a user id so you can come back to the same account.
  • We save the streaming services you choose and your latest tonight answers so the picker can pick up where you left off.
  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (email/password or Google).

Movie and streaming data

Titles, posters, and ratings come from TMDB. Where a title is available to stream is informed by JustWatch. Streamly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by TMDB, JustWatch, or any streaming service.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t show ads or run trackers for advertising.
  • We don’t ask for payment information.

Questions

If you want your account data deleted, sign in and use Sign out for now, or reach out from the product site. We’ll remove the stored profile, services, and tonight answers for that account.
